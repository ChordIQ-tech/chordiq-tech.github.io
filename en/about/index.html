Why we exist

Purpose
To join engineering and AI to optimize industrial processes from data and science, turning physical knowledge into decisions that are faster, safer and more profitable.
How
We start from the equations that already govern your process and use machine learning only where theory alone does not close the gap: an unknown parameter, a real geometry, a regime outside the catalog. Every delivery shows the error measured against the reference, never a promise without a number.
Vision
To be the reference in Physics AI, the company people remember when industry needs to join simulation, data and AI with scientific rigour.

Positioning

We are not a generic AI consultancy or a software house.
We are applied engineering with scientific AI, built to measure for each client's process.

Sunny day, stable market

Impossible to overtake

On a dry track with uniform grip, it is practically impossible to overtake a pack of 20 Formula 1 cars. Tech giants dominate the same way: consolidated scale, capital and infrastructure make direct competition nearly unviable for a small company.

Rainy day, disrupted market

The game changes completely

All it takes is rain for the concept to change: unpredictable grip and strategy matter more than raw power, and overtaking becomes frequent. In technological disruption, an agile pair like ChordIQ and Domus is more efficient and has less to lose, thanks to the agility of the three-phase model.

We don't compete head-on with the giants on a sunny day. We were designed to win when it rains.
