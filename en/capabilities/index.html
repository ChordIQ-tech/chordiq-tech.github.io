Surrogate models from physics

From heavy simulation to a model that answers in microseconds

We train a surrogate on the real physics of your process and check it against the original simulation at every delivery, never against itself.

Digital twins

Process automation and optimization

From sensor to operating point, with an agent in the middle

Plant readings, a physical model and an agent that suggests (or adjusts) the setpoint, always within the safety limits your team defines.

Capability
Design experimentation

Dozens of geometry variants, a single sweep

Surrogate-guided optimization over the design space, with each final variant re-checked against the physical model before it becomes a recommendation.

Capability
Parameter discovery from data

The property nobody measured, estimated from what already exists

Bayesian inversion (Ensemble Kalman) or SINDy to recover physical parameters and even governing equations from sensor readings that are already installed.

Capability
Model architecture benchmark

Which network solves your problem best, not the paper's

An honest comparison between architectures (PINN, FNO, physical Transformer, MLP) on the same data and training budget, including when our choice does not win.

Capability
Time-step forecasting

From the state now to the state tomorrow, step by step, without diverging

Models that advance the field in time using their own predictions as input, trained not to accumulate error. The useful horizon is measured against persistence and climatology, never promised.

Capability
Adaptive model ensemble

The right model for each case, chosen while the operation runs

Neural operators, graph networks, PINNs and solvers in the same ensemble: the weights learn from the error, or from the physics residual when there is no measurement, and a noisy sensor reading does not knock the choice over.

In practice

The real call behind 4 of our deliverables.

A service without auditable code is a promise, not a deliverable. This is not illustrative pseudocode: these are real signatures from our own libraries and endpoints.

Digital twin · surrogate model 
# trains the surrogate and checks it against a real 3D simulation
surrogate, _ = engine.train_surrogate()
resultado = engine.run_sweep(surrogate)
# result: temperature error vs. FEA, per design

Real output: 4.46% mean temperature error against a 3D finite-element simulation, 227,000× faster.

Verification · physics before the decision 
# checks physical consistency and convergence, not just whether it "solved"
from pinneapple_analysis.verification import physics_confidence_score as pcs

confianca = pcs.compute_physics_confidence(
    guardrail_report=guardrail_report,
    convergence_result=convergence_result,
)

Real output: a score from 0 to 1 per check, never an average that hides a check that did not run.

Flow measurement · software PIV 
# same algorithm as the /v1/analyze_sequence endpoint, called directly
from pinneapple_perception.video_piv import piv_velocity_field

campo = piv_velocity_field(
    frame_a, frame_b, window_size=32, search_margin=12, dt=1.0,
)
# campo["u"], campo["v"]: velocity per interrogation window

Real output: 81 velocity vectors recovered, 3.94% mean error against the exact field.

Interoperability · mesh conversion and repair 
# real production endpoint, a real multipart upload
curl -X POST https://api.../v1/convert \
  -F "file=@malha_legada.stl" \
  -F "output=stl" -F "repair=true"
# response: mesh with no duplicates, no degenerate faces, normals ok

Real output: 25 of 25 defective faces removed, normals 100% consistent after the repair.

3D visualization of any result

Results from OpenFOAM, CalculiX, Abaqus, Gmsh or VTK become 3D scenes (glTF, USD for Omniverse, a web viewer and Blender renders). Below, the Ahmed body, a vehicle-aerodynamics reference: OpenFOAM flow (RANS k-ω SST, 40 m/s, 25° slant), with CD of 0.298 against about 0.285 measured, on a medium mesh of 0.20 million cells and 10 minutes on 2 cores.
