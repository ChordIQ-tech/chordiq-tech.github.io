HeatSink Sizer

Heat-sink sizing in seconds, with the confidence stated.

The problem

Sizing a heat sink usually goes the usual way: spreadsheet, educated guess, slow simulation, tweak, simulate again.

How it works

You enter the component's power, its maximum allowed temperature, the space available and the cooling mode (natural or forced air). The tool screens thousands of fin geometries with a neural surrogate trained on the physics and verifies the best candidate with published, classical heat-transfer correlations.

Measured results

  • Temperature, pressure drop and thermal margin, with a 3D view of the heat sink and its temperature field
  • Physics cross-checked against independent methods from the literature, agreeing within 0–5%
  • A surrogate with 4.5% mean error against 3D finite-element simulation, on cases it never saw in training
  • States how far the model can be trusted and what it doesn't cover yet
