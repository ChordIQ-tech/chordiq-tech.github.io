Simulation Interoperability Hub

Simulation results in one neutral dataset, with the units kept.

The problem

One script per pair of formats, and the units get lost on the way.

How it works

Upload an OpenFOAM, CalculiX, VTK or Gmsh result and get one neutral physical dataset: geometry, mesh, fields with quantity and unit, and metadata. Then export to VTK, HDF5, Parquet, CSV, NPZ, JSON or a PINNeAPPle dataset, ready to train models on.

Measured results

  • OpenFOAM to VTU, reread by VTK with the same volume checkMesh reports
