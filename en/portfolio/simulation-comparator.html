Simulation Comparator

How far apart are two results, field by field and point by point?

The problem

Comparing a simulation with another one, with an experiment or with an AI model usually turns into a one-off script, and comparing across different meshes is the easiest part to get wrong.

How it works

Reference vs candidate: simulation/simulation, simulation/experiment, simulation/AI, AI/experiment. The tool returns global and per-field error and an error map, even on different meshes.

Measured results

  • Lid-driven cavity vs Ghia et al.: error from 1.26% to 0.19% with mesh refinement
  • A PINN vs finite volumes: 0.39% error (max 0.67 °C)
