Simulation Preflight

Every problem in a case flagged before you run the simulation.

The problem

How many hours have you lost to a simulation that ran to the end and was wrong from the start?

How it works

Upload an OpenFOAM case or a CalculiX deck and get an exportable checklist with PASS, WARNING or FAIL, the file and line, why it matters and how to fix it.

Measured results

  • A beam with no supports: CalculiX says "Job finished" and reports a displacement of 1.8e11 mm; Preflight flags it as FAIL
  • Mixed units: the first frequency comes out at 0.16 Hz instead of 209 Hz; Preflight catches it before the run
